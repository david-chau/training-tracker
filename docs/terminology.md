# Training terminology

Short definitions for the terms used throughout the tracker.

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Rep / reps** (repetition) | One complete movement of an exercise. One squat is one rep; `8 reps` means eight squats in a row. |
| **Set** | A group of reps performed together before resting. `3 sets of 8` means do eight reps, rest, and repeat twice. |
| **Weight** | The load used for a set, shown in pounds (lb) by default. It can include a barbell, dumbbells, a machine setting, or added weight. |
| **RPE** (rate of perceived exertion) | Your rating of how hard a set felt, from 1 to 10. In this tracker, around 7–8.5 means a productive working set; 10 means you could not have completed another rep. RPE helps propose the next session's reps and weight. |
| **PR** (personal record) | Your best result for an exercise according to a chosen measure. The tracker can flag a heaviest set, most reps, best volume, or estimated one-rep max. See [Personal records](records.html) for the exact rules. |
| **1RM** (one-rep max) | The heaviest weight you can lift once for an exercise. **Estimated 1RM** is a calculation based on a set with multiple reps, not necessarily a lift you have tested. |
| **Volume** | The total work in a set: reps × weight. For example, 8 reps at 100 lb has a volume of 800 lb. |
| **Bodyweight exercise** | An exercise where you move your body rather than a separately recorded load, such as a push-up or plank. These can be set to `no weight`; the tracker then focuses on reps. |
| **Progression** | The suggested adjustment for a future session based on the previous set's RPE—usually more reps, more weight, or a repeat. It is a proposal, not a requirement. |

## Reading a logged set

`8 × 100 @ RPE 8` means: eight reps, using 100 lb, and it felt like an 8 out
of 10 for effort.
